Traditional Dress Code

  • • Traditional ethnic Indian attire strongly encouraged.
  • • Chaniya Choli, Kurta Pajama, or festive regional wear.

05 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Need immediate assistance?
Call +91 70505 51310 or WhatsApp us directly.

How do I book a ticket and get my Pass ID?

Click "Book Tickets" on this page, choose Solo (₹249) or Couple (₹399), fill in your name and contact details, and scan the official PhonePe QR code to pay. Once paid, click "Generate Pass" to instantly receive your unique Pass ID (e.g. JMU-7K29) and Digital Entry Pass!

How does the WhatsApp payment screenshot verification work?

After your Pass is generated, click the green "Send on WhatsApp" button. A pre-filled message with your Pass ID and registration details will open on WhatsApp to +91 70505 51310. Simply attach your payment screenshot in that chat so the organizer can verify your booking.

Who can use the Couple Ticket (₹399)?

The ₹399 Couple Ticket is reserved exclusively for a married couple, as stipulated by the organizers. A Solo Ticket costs ₹249 per individual attendee.

What if I close my browser? How do I retrieve my Pass?

Your Pass is automatically saved to your device's browser. You can click "My Pass" anytime and enter your unique Ticket ID (e.g. JMU-7K29) to view or download your digital entry pass.

Is food or Dandiya sticks included in the pass?

The pass covers your official festival entry to the Dandiya and Jhijhiya celebration arena. Attendees may bring their own decorated Dandiya sticks. Authentic food and beverage stalls will be available inside the venue for individual purchase.
